# NST Enterprises — Industrial B2B Catalogue & Enquiry Platform

A production-ready B2B industrial and agricultural metal product catalogue and enquiry platform developed for **NST Enterprises**, an established industrial and agricultural metal supplier based in IDA Jeedimetla, Hyderabad, Telangana.

## About the Project

NST Enterprises required a modern digital presence where customers could:

- Explore industrial and agricultural products
- View product images and specifications
- Select relevant product requirements
- Submit quotation enquiries
- Contact the business directly through WhatsApp and phone
- Find the physical facility in Hyderabad

The platform is designed around a **catalogue + enquiry workflow** rather than fixed online pricing, allowing customers to submit requirements for products whose pricing depends on dimensions, thickness, quantity and other requirements.
